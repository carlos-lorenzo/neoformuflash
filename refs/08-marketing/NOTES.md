# 08 Marketing & Landing Pages

This folder defines the mechanical rules for high-converting SaaS landing pages, emphasizing product-led composition, frictionless interactive demos, and bold quantitative proof.

*   **The "Show, Don't Tell" Composition (`authkit_hero.jpg`, `increase.com_feature_display_2.jpg`, `interfere.com_feature_display.jpg`):** Modern SaaS marketing relies heavily on displaying the actual product UI rather than abstract illustrations. 
    *   The AuthKit hero features a distinctly minimalist and modern aesthetic. It utilizes a concise hero section that delivers the value proposition with clarity.
    *   Interfere highlights how it watches everything your app ships and turns the noise into a short list of real problems. It achieves this visually by showing the exact code and priority UI directly in the feature cards.
    *   Increase showcases its API-first core technology by juxtaposing physical cards with code snippets and clean account graphs.
*   **Interactive Product Previews (`atio_livedemo.jpg`):** To ease the pain point of committing to a signup, landing pages are integrating interactive sandboxes. 
    *   Attio utilizes animated product previews that foreground the SaaS interface in real-world scenarios.
    *   This specific design choice is highly effective at instilling trust and curiosity in potential users. 
*   **Quantitative Stats Showcase (`stats_x.jpg`):** When communicating platform effectiveness, data is elevated to the primary visual element. 
    *   The X Ads platform emphasizes that it is built for business impact and backed by measurable results.
    *   To prove this, the layout uses massive typography in an asymmetric grid to highlight specific metrics, such as a 4.74x incremental lift and 85% net new customers.
